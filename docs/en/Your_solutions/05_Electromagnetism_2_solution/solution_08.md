To order these types of electromagnetic radiation correctly, it is helpful to remember that **frequency is inversely proportional to wavelength**. Therefore, radiation with the highest energy and frequency will have the shortest wavelength.

### Order: Increasing Wavelength

To list them from shortest wavelength to longest wavelength, we go from the highest energy (most dangerous/penetrative) to the lowest energy:

1. **Gamma rays** (Shortest wavelength)
2. **X-rays**
3. **Ultraviolet**
4. **Infrared**
5. **Microwaves**
6. **Radio waves** (Longest wavelength)

---

### Concept Summary

The electromagnetic spectrum is organized by the energy of the photons.

* **High Energy / High Frequency:** Gamma rays are on the high-energy end of the spectrum with extremely short wavelengths (often smaller than an atom).
* **Low Energy / Low Frequency:** Radio waves are on the low-energy end of the spectrum, with wavelengths that can range from the length of a football field to much longer.
