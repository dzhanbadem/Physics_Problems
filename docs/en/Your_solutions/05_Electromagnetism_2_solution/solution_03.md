To solve this problem, we will use the Biot-Savart Law, which describes the magnetic field produced by a short, infinitesimal current segment.

### Given Information

* **Current ($I$):** $3.0\text{ A}$
* **Length of segment ($dl$):** $0.1\text{ m}$
* **Distance to point $P$ ($r$):** $0.2\text{ m}$
* **Angle ($\theta$):** Since the segment is perpendicular to the line connecting it to point $P$, $\theta = 90^\circ$ (or $\sin\theta = 1$).
* **Constant:** $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$.

### Concepts & Formulas

1. **Biot-Savart Law:** The magnetic field $dB$ produced by a small current element $I d\vec{l}$ at a distance $r$ is given by:

$$dB = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{I \, dl \sin\theta}{r^2}$$



*Note: We can use this formula because the segment length $dl$ is small relative to the distance $r$.*

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Identify the values**

* $\mu_0 / 4\pi = 10^{-7}\text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$
* $I = 3\text{ A}$
* $dl = 0.1\text{ m}$
* $r = 0.2\text{ m}$
* $\sin(90^\circ) = 1$

**Step 2: Substitute into the Biot-Savart formula**


$$dB = (10^{-7}\text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}) \cdot \frac{(3\text{ A}) \cdot (0.1\text{ m}) \cdot 1}{(0.2\text{ m})^2}$$

**Step 3: Perform the calculation**
First, calculate the denominator:


$$r^2 = (0.2)^2 = 0.04\text{ m}^2$$

Now, solve for $dB$:


$$dB = 10^{-7} \cdot \frac{0.3}{0.04}$$

$$dB = 10^{-7} \cdot 7.5$$

$$dB = 7.5 \times 10^{-7}\text{ T}$$

### Final Answer

The magnitude of the magnetic field at point $P$ is **$7.5 \times 10^{-7}\text{ T}$** (or $0.75\text{ \mu T}$).

*Regarding direction: By the Right-Hand Rule, if the current segment points upward and the point $P$ is to the right, the magnetic field at point $P$ will point **into the page**.*
