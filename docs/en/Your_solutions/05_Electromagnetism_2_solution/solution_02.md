To solve this problem, we will use the Biot-Savart Law (specifically the formula for the magnetic field created by a long straight wire) and the Principle of Superposition for magnetic fields.

### Given Information

* **Currents ($I_1, I_2$):** Both are $5.0 \text{ A}$, but in opposite directions.
* **Separation distance ($d$):** $10 \text{ cm} = 0.1 \text{ m}$.
* **Point of interest ($P$):** Midway between the wires, so $r_1 = r_2 = 0.05 \text{ m}$.
* **Constant:** Permeability of free space $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$.

### Concepts & Formulas

1. **Magnetic Field of a Long Wire:** The magnitude of the magnetic field $B$ at a distance $r$ from a long, straight current-carrying wire is:

$$B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$$


2. **Right-Hand Rule:** To find the direction of the magnetic field, point your right thumb in the direction of the current; your fingers will curl in the direction of the magnetic field lines.
3. **Superposition Principle:** The total magnetic field is the vector sum of the fields produced by each wire: $\vec{B}_{\text{net}} = \vec{B}_1 + \vec{B}_2$.

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Calculate the magnitude of the field from each wire**
For wire 1 ($I_1 = 5 \text{ A}$):


$$B_1 = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A})(5 \text{ A})}{2\pi (0.05 \text{ m})}$$

$$B_1 = \frac{2 \times 10^{-7} \times 5}{0.05} = 2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T}$$

Since wire 2 also carries $5 \text{ A}$ at the same distance ($0.05 \text{ m}$), $B_2$ has the same magnitude:


$$B_2 = 2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T}$$

**Step 2: Determine the direction of the fields at the midpoint**
Imagine the wires are horizontal. Let Wire 1 carry current to the right and Wire 2 carry current to the left.

* **Wire 1 (right):** Using the right-hand rule, at the midpoint *above* the wire, the field points out of the page. Since our point is *between* the wires, we use the right-hand rule to find the field points **into the page**.
* **Wire 2 (left):** Using the right-hand rule, at the midpoint, the field also points **into the page**.

Because both currents produce fields pointing in the same direction at the midpoint, we add their magnitudes.

**Step 3: Calculate the total magnetic field**


$$B_{\text{net}} = B_1 + B_2$$

$$B_{\text{net}} = 2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T} + 2 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T} = 4 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T}$$

### Final Answer

The magnitude of the magnetic field at the midpoint is **$4.0 \times 10^{-5} \text{ T}$**, and the direction is **into the plane** containing the two wires (assuming the wires lie in the plane of the page).
