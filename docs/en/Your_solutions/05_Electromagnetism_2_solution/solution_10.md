To solve this, we will use the definition of the refractive index, which relates the speed of light in a vacuum to its speed in a specific medium.

### Given Information

* **Index of refraction of diamond ($n$):** $2.42$
* **Speed of light in a vacuum ($c$):** $\approx 3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$

---

### Concepts & Formulas

1. **Refractive Index Definition:** The index of refraction ($n$) of a medium is defined as the ratio of the speed of light in a vacuum ($c$) to the speed of light in that medium ($v$):

$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$



Rearranging this to solve for the speed in the medium ($v$):

$$v = \frac{c}{n}$$



---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Set up the equation**
Using the formula $v = c / n$:


$$v = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{2.42}$$

**Step 2: Perform the calculation**


$$v \approx 1.2396 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$$
