To solve this problem, we will treat the system as an ideal parallel-plate capacitor.

### Given Information

* **Area ($S$):** $0.02 \, \text{m}^2$
* **Plate separation ($d$):** $5 \, \text{mm} = 0.005 \, \text{m}$
* **Voltage ($U$):** $500 \, \text{V}$
* **Permittivity of free space ($\varepsilon_0$):** $\approx 8.854 \times 10^{-12} \, \text{F/m}$

---

### 1. Capacitance ($C$)

The capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor is given by $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 S}{d}$.

**Calculation:**


$$C = \frac{(8.854 \times 10^{-12} \, \text{F/m}) \times 0.02 \, \text{m}^2}{0.005 \, \text{m}}$$

$$C = 8.854 \times 10^{-12} \times 4 = 3.5416 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{F}$$


**Result:** $C \approx 35.42 \, \text{pF}$ (picofarads).

---

### 2. Energy Stored ($W$)

The energy $W$ stored in a capacitor is given by $W = \frac{1}{2} C U^2$.

**Calculation:**


$$W = 0.5 \times (3.5416 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{F}) \times (500 \, \text{V})^2$$

$$W = 0.5 \times 3.5416 \times 10^{-11} \times 250,000$$

$$W = 4.427 \times 10^{-6} \, \text{J}$$


**Result:** $W \approx 4.43 \, \mu\text{J}$ (microjoules).

---

### 3. Electric Field Intensity ($E$)

For a parallel-plate capacitor, the electric field is uniform and defined by $E = \frac{U}{d}$.

**Calculation:**


$$E = \frac{500 \, \text{V}}{0.005 \, \text{m}}$$

$$E = 100,000 \, \text{V/m}$$


**Result:** $E = 1.0 \times 10^5 \, \text{V/m}$ (or $\text{N/C}$).

---

### 4. Force of Attraction ($F$)

The plates attract each other because they carry opposite charges. The force $F$ can be derived from the energy or the electric field as $F = \frac{1}{2} \varepsilon_0 E^2 S$ or $F = \frac{Q^2}{2 \varepsilon_0 S}$.

**Calculation using $E$:**


$$F = 0.5 \times (8.854 \times 10^{-12} \, \text{F/m}) \times (10^5 \, \text{V/m})^2 \times 0.02 \, \text{m}^2$$

$$F = 0.5 \times 8.854 \times 10^{-12} \times 10^{10} \times 0.02$$

$$F = 8.854 \times 10^{-4} \, \text{N}$$


**Result:** $F \approx 8.85 \times 10^{-4} \, \text{N}$.
