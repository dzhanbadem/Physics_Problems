To determine the properties of this light, we will use the wave speed equation for electromagnetic radiation.

### Given Information

* **Wavelength ($\lambda$):** $550 \text{ nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$
* **Speed of light ($c$):** $\approx 3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$

---

### 1. Identifying the Color

In the visible light spectrum, wavelengths range approximately from $380 \text{ nm}$ (violet) to $750 \text{ nm}$ (red).

* $550 \text{ nm}$ falls squarely in the middle of the visible range.
* This specific wavelength corresponds to **yellow-green** light, to which the human eye's cone cells are maximally sensitive in daylight conditions.

### 2. Calculating the Frequency ($f$)

The relationship between the speed of light ($c$), frequency ($f$), and wavelength ($\lambda$) is given by:


$$c = f \lambda \implies f = \frac{c}{\lambda}$$

**Step-by-Step Calculation:**

1. Substitute the known values into the equation:

$$f = \frac{3.00 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}}$$


2. Simplify the exponent:

$$f = \frac{3.00}{550} \times 10^{17} \text{ Hz}$$


$$f \approx 0.00545 \times 10^{17} \text{ Hz}$$


3. Convert to standard scientific notation:

$$f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$$



### Final Answer

* The color corresponds to **yellow-green**.
* The frequency of this light is approximately **$5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$** (or $545 \text{ THz}$).
