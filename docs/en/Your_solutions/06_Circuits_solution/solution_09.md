To find the current $I$ at a specific time, we use the definition that electric current is the rate of change of charge with respect to time:

$$I(t) = \frac{dQ(t)}{dt}$$

### Given Information

* **Charge Function:** $Q(t) = 5t^2 + 5$
* **Time ($t$):** $3 \, \text{s}$

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Differentiate the charge function with respect to time**
We apply the power rule for differentiation ($\frac{d}{dt}(at^n) = ant^{n-1}$):


$$\frac{d}{dt}(5t^2 + 5) = 10t + 0$$


So, the expression for current as a function of time is:


$$I(t) = 10t$$

**Step 2: Evaluate at $t = 3 \, \text{s}$**
Substitute $3$ for $t$ in our current equation:


$$I(3) = 10 \times 3$$

$$I(3) = 30 \, \text{A}$$

### Final Answer

The current at $t = 3 \, \text{s}$ is **$30 \, \text{A}$**.
