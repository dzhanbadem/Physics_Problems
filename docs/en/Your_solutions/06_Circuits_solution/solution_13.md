To determine the number of turns required for the secondary coil, we use the **Transformer Turns Ratio** formula.

### Given Information

* **Primary voltage ($V_p$):** $120\,\text{V}$
* **Secondary voltage ($V_s$):** $9.0\,\text{V}$
* **Primary turns ($N_p$):** $400$
* **Target:** Secondary turns ($N_s$)

---

### Concept & Formula

The ratio of voltages in a transformer is directly proportional to the ratio of the number of turns in the coils:


$$\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}$$

Rearranging the formula to solve for $N_s$:


$$N_s = N_p \times \left( \frac{V_s}{V_p} \right)$$

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Set up the calculation**
Substitute the known values into the rearranged formula:


$$N_s = 400 \times \left( \frac{9.0\,\text{V}}{120\,\text{V}} \right)$$

**Step 2: Solve the ratio**
First, calculate the ratio of the voltages:


$$\frac{9.0}{120} = 0.075$$

**Step 3: Calculate the number of turns**


$$N_s = 400 \times 0.075 = 30$$

### Final Answer

The secondary coil must have **$30$ turns**.
